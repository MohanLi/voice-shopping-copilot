# The private catalog

Generated by `voiceshop eval proofagent` from the index the assistant runs on.

- Source: Amazon Product Dataset 2020, cleaned. 9,741 products from 2,204 brands.
- Largest categories: Toys & Games (6,522), Home & Kitchen (688), Clothing, Shoes & Jewelry (628), Sports & Outdoors (475), Baby Products (214), Arts, Crafts & Sewing (117), Office Products (68). It is mostly toys and games.
- Prices are in US dollars and come from a January 2020 snapshot (median $16.99). They are called catalog prices. A price is called current only when a live web result states it.
- The catalog has **no customer ratings and no review counts**. A rating appears in an answer only when a live web listing states one.
- The catalog has no stock or availability information. Availability can only come from a live web result that states it.
- Brand, product type and materials were extracted from each listing's own text; nothing was added from outside knowledge. The Ingredients column shows the materials or ingredients a source states, or "not stated".
- Age recommendations come from the listing. For children under three the assistant only returns products whose listing says they suit babies or toddlers, because the dataset's age field proved unreliable below three years.
- Each catalog product is cited by its document id (for example `AMZ-B000B9RHYC`).
- The catalog does not contain electronics, groceries, medicines, cleaning chemicals or most household consumables; for those the assistant can only use live web results.

# Live web search

- Provider chain for this run: fault-injection, fixture. Results are cached for 180 seconds.
- Shopping listings state a title, a store, a price and sometimes a star rating. They carry no description, so they do not say what a product is made of or whether it is in stock.
- Web pages (product pages, reviews) state properties in their snippet; they usually state no price.
- A live result is cited by its URL and the store or site name, with the time it was retrieved.
