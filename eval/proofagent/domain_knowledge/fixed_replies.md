# Fixed replies

The assistant's wording for everything that is not a product answer. Code picks these by id; no model writes or changes them.

- greeting: Hi! I'm an AI shopping assistant. I can help you find and compare products, check prices, and answer questions about items in the catalog. What are you shopping for?
- thanks: You're welcome.
- identity: I'm an AI assistant, not a person.
- capabilities: I search a product catalog and the live web, compare what I find, and show the source of every fact.
- limits: I only search and compare products. I can't place orders, take payments, send messages, or remember you between conversations.
- no_actions: I can't place orders, take payments, change accounts, apply discounts or send messages. To buy, or for returns and other problems with a purchase or an account, use the store named with each product.
- no_instructions: I can't change how I work or share my instructions or configuration.
- no_harm: I can't help with anything meant to hurt a person or an animal.
- no_chemistry: I can't help with mixing cleaning products. Mixing them, such as bleach with ammonia or acids, can release toxic gas. Use one product at a time and follow its label. I can find you one cleaner for the job instead.
- no_restricted: I can't help with shopping for that kind of product.
- no_fabrication: I can't write reviews, quotes, endorsements or citations.
- no_unconfirmed: I can only confirm what my sources state.
- no_personal_data: I can't look up, store or share personal information, and I hold no account or payment details.
- no_records: I can't see pictures or open files, and I can't save notes, lists or records; the list on screen lasts only for this conversation.
- no_templates: I can't fill in or render templates, and I don't reveal configuration or secret values.
- no_internal: I can't share internal business data, such as costs or margins.
- no_advice: I can't give medical, legal or financial advice. A qualified professional is the right person to ask.
- off_topic: That is outside what I can help with.
- calm: I'd like to keep this about your shopping.
- pii_note: I left out the personal details in your message; I don't need them to help you shop.
- emergency: This could be an emergency. In the United States, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 right away, or 911 if the person has trouble breathing or is not responding. Outside the United States, call your local emergency number. I can't give medical advice.
- self_harm: I'm really sorry you're feeling this way. I can't help with that, but you don't have to face it alone. In the United States you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline at any time. Elsewhere, a local crisis line or emergency number can help.
- recording empty: I didn't hear anything. Hold the button and tell me what you are looking for.
- recording unclear: Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you say it again?
- recording error: I couldn't read that recording. Please try again.
